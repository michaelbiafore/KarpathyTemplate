# Deep Research Agents

There are just too many great things to be found in this paper that are worthwhile
to explore, so we would urge anyone reading this to take a stab at exploring the
paper; it’s a great read! Likewise, it’s a great example of the agent structure we covered
in the first part of the book, with additional social interactions and communication
structures for simulated environments.
Since the release of this paper, there have been many other simulacra that serve
purposes other than purely social interactions. Agent Hospital, for example, takes
inspiration from Smallville and is a hospital sandbox that simulates the entire patient
journey, from disease onset and triage all the way to the medicine dispensary and
post-hospital follow-up visit.20 In this simulation, doctor agents can evolve as they
treat many patient agents, thereby improving their capabilities. SimClass, likewise,
simulates classroom education with agent-student and student-student interactions.21
Emerging behavior shows collaboration as student agents work together to solve
tasks. These kinds of simulations are great case studies for how agents might interact
and collaborate as tasks differ and grow in complexity.
Deep Research Agents
A great use case for systems that leverage multiple agents is Deep Research. Deep
Research is a system where agents work together to research topics and provide
comprehensive reports using various resources. In 2025, it gained significant trac‐
tion, with large AI-driven organizations such as Anthropic, Perplexity, Google, and
OpenAI adopting Deep Research products.
We covered single Deep Research agents to an extent in Chapters 3, 4, and 5, such as
Search-o1 and Search-R1, due to their heavy reliance on tools, memory, and reason‐
ing. MASs, however, are particularly useful for Deep Research due to the complexity
of the use cases and the collaborative nature of scientific research.22 Anthropic’s
multi-agent research system, for instance, has search agents, a citation agent, and a
lead researcher agent to orchestrate the workflow.
In this section, we cover two recent MASs for Deep Research agents. These systems
are great examples of the intricate nature of proper research and how to build
networks of agents that can do so.
