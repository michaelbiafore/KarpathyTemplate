# Colophon

Colophon
The animal on the cover of An Illustrated Guide to AI Agents is a common bottle‐
nose dolphin (Tursiops truncatus), a charismatic, adaptable mammal found globally
throughout temperate and tropical waters. Their range includes shallow bays and
estuaries as well as deep open oceans. Coastal populations tend to be smaller and
lighter, while offshore individuals are more robust. On average, adults span from 6 to
13 feet long and weigh between 300 and 1,400 pounds.
Bottlenose dolphins eat a variety of fish, squid, and crustaceans to sustain their
high energy demands. They are highly social creatures, living in fluid, ever-changing
groups called pods of between 2 and 15 individuals. Within their pods, dolphins
display complex cooperative behaviors, such as working together to herd or trap
schools of fish. They are playful and communicate with whistles, clicks, echolocation,
and body language.
The average lifespan of bottlenose dolphins is 40 to 60 years. The IUCN Red List of
Threatened Species last assessed common bottlenose dolphins in 2018 and lists them
as Least Concern. Many of the animals on O’Reilly covers are endangered; all of them
are important to the world.
